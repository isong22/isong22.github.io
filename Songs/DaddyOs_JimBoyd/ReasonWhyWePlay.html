Reason Why We Play

Key : E?   | 4/4 slow stomp

You must’a heard us holler from, The bottom where we been
And yeah, we had to waller some, Before we could get up again
Now we’re headed back to the high notes, Gonna meet in the middle when
We catch up to that kick drum, And find some strings to bend.

Chorus:
In the pines (in the pines), Way out in the Georgia pines
We’re playing it with feeling, And now we’re feeling fine
The reason why we play is, The reason that we breathe or eat
We got this music in our blood, And our heart thumps to the beat.

You know we got it sounding right, When everybody’s right on time
And yeah, we got it wound up tight, And we’re nailing every line
When the people out there all join in, Same reason and same rhyme
Everybody in the house is in this band, Way out in the Georgia pines.

Chorus

Bridge: And you know we’re feeling proud, When we look out on a crowd
And hear ‘em shout out loud “Man, this music kills!”

You know we all gonna sing out. Some words been runnin’ ‘round
And yeah, we gotta bring out, All these tricks and licks we found
For a while we gonna lay back, Let it ride and bring it down
But soon someone will stray back, And the boys are back in town.

Chorus

Note: For all the bands, incarnations, and the guys still with us.
